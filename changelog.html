What's new.

Recent changes for Spatch patches and Spatch Manager, latest first. Full release notes and APK downloads live on GitHub.

Patches

Artwork pulse option, larger landscape art, and single-pass playlists

Added an Artwork pulse toggle in Player settings, enlarged landscape player artwork with proportional visualizer ring stroke thickness, sped up playlist header shapes with a cover-tap bounce and spin boost, and removed double rebuilds when opening cached playlists and albums.

Patches

Landscape lyrics switcher, smoother Library tabs, and Sleep countdown ring

Added automatic switching between Lyrics and Up next in the opt-in landscape player based on lyric availability, rebuilt Library filter chips into a sliding capsule without scroll jumps alongside a single-row landscape toolbar, arranged the landscape Create popup into a two-column sheet, enforced portrait lock when Allow rotation is off, animated three slowly spinning header shapes behind playlist covers, added tap-to-spin on Home's greeting icon, and added a circular countdown ring to the active Sleep timer sheet.

Patches

Sliding segmented pills and two-pane landscape Search

Updated segmented option groups across Search and Settings with an animated overshoot indicator pill, added a side-by-side two-pane landscape Search layout with spring press feedback on recent queries, and extended shared cover flight transitions to Search albums, playlists, and artists.

Patches

10-band EQ faders and two-pane landscape playlists

Added a switchable 10-band vertical graphic faders view with relative delta-drag control and live dB pills in Equalizer and sound, plus a two-pane landscape layout and header scroll parallax on playlist and album pages.

Patches

Shared cover flight, miniature theme previews, and heart bursts

Added shared-element cover flight transitions into playlist and album pages, miniature app-window palette previews in Appearance, an animated overshoot pill indicator on landscape Lyrics and Up next tabs, and a burst of hearts when saving the current song.

Patches

Contour visualizer mode, delta-drag EQ, and greeting badge

Added a radial Contour visualizer option alongside Bars and Ring, relative delta-drag gestures on the equalizer curve, and a time-of-day shape badge beside the Home greeting.

Patches

Bass cover pulse, header stickers, and landscape Home and Library

Added bass-reactive cover breathing inside the visualizer ring, dual-colour cover glows, deterministic background stickers behind playlist and album headers, a floating seek-time pill with track-change scrub guard, four-column landscape Library grids, and side-by-side landscape Home quick cards.

Patches

Opt-in direct audio read, two-tone cover palettes, and Gaussian EQ

Added an opt-in direct audio read from Spotify's output stream without microphone permission, extracted distinct primary and secondary accents from multi-colour album covers, and shaped sound on a continuous Gaussian-summed equalizer curve with a live dB badge.

Patches

Landscape Up next tabs, idle-fade picker, and Sleep timer adjustments

Switch between synced lyrics and the Up next queue beside the cover in landscape, pick which player elements fade when idle, restart cleanly on Previous at 0:00, and adjust an active sleep timer by ±5 minutes with a live countdown.

Patches

Widget clipping, softer cover glow, and zero idle frames

Fixed widget corner clipping and text readability shadows, smoothed circular visualizer proportions across landscape screens and removed abrupt glow rings, and stopped idle render wakeups when playback is paused.

Patches

Time-synced lyrics screen, player lyric strip, and calmer top bar

Added a time-synced lyrics screen where the sung line is bright, the column glides to keep it centred, and tapping a line jumps the song there. Added an optional two-line lyric strip in the player (one to three lines, a setting) over a soft cover blur, a calmer player top bar with a more menu, and settings for every piece of the player.

Patches

Adaptive home-screen widgets and Quick Settings tile

Widgets keep their cover square and sized to the space you give them, with a stacked layout for tall widgets. Added a Quick Settings play/pause tile.

Patches

Spotify 9.2.0.2053 target and SHA-256 verification

Built for Spotify for Android 9.2.0.2053 (version code 148387875) as an APKM bundle. Every input APKM, split APK, and patched entry is checked against its exact SHA-256.

Manager

Clones, nightly patches, and live build progress

Install a second Spotify clone beside the first with its own package name, app name, and adaptive launcher icon. Opt into nightly patches with live build progress, self-updates, and one-tap base.apk updates (silent on Android 12 or newer).
