Questions, answered.

What to know about safety, accounts, supported versions, signing keys, and settings.

Safety and account

Is Spatch safe?

Spatch is open source: everything it changes is in the repository, and the Manager checks every file against its SHA-256. It needs no account and sends nothing anywhere. It is still a modified app, so it cannot come with Spotify's guarantees.

Can my account get banned?

Modified clients are against Spotify's terms. Spatch only changes how the app looks and does not unlock anything, so the risk is low, but it is not zero. Use it at your own risk.

Does Spatch remove ads or unlock Premium?

No. Spatch is cosmetic by design. It does not skip, block, or hide ads, and it does not unlock Premium features. On free accounts it only makes sure its own artwork does not fight with Spotify's ad cards.

Does Spatch work on free accounts?

Yes. Free accounts can also hide the Premium tab.

Versions and signing keys

Why do I need a specific Spotify version?

Spatch edits Spotify's own code in exact places. Each Spotify release moves those places, so every version needs its own patches. Spatch is built for Spotify 9.2.0.2053 (version code 148387875) and refuses anything it was not built for instead of patching blindly.

Why does Spotify have to be uninstalled the first time?

Android only updates an app with one signed by the same key. Spatch is signed with your phone's key, not Spotify's, so the original has to go first. After that every update installs in place.

What happens if I lose my key?

You would have to uninstall Spatch to install a build signed by a new key, losing downloads. Export the key from the Manager and keep it safe.

Can I go back to normal Spotify?

Uninstall Spatch and install Spotify from the Play Store.

Using Spatch

Where are Spatch's settings?

Open Spotify's side panel (your profile picture) and choose Spatch, or open the calmer player top bar's more menu to reach player settings. Every piece of the player is a setting, so you can keep only what you want.

Does the visualizer need microphone permission?

No, when you turn on the opt-in direct audio read setting. It reads audio straight from Spotify's output without asking for microphone permission.

What can I turn on in the player, widgets, and Quick Settings?

The time-synced lyrics screen lights up the sung line, glides the column to keep it centred, and lets you tap a line to jump there. You can also turn on an optional two-line lyric strip in the player (one to three lines, a setting) and an opt-in landscape player.

Home-screen widgets keep the cover square and sized to the space you give them, switching to a stacked layout when tall. A Quick Settings tile handles play and pause without opening the app.

Who makes Spatch?

Spatch is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify AB.
