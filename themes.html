Themes

18 palettes, one tap

Every theme recolours the player, lists, cards, and Spotify's own surfaces. Switching applies straight away or after a quick restart, and your choice survives updates.

Dynamic & custom

Wallpaper colours, or your own

Not limited to presets—Spatch can follow your phone's Material You palette or take any custom colours you set.

Wallpaper palette (Android 12+)

Your phone's Material You palette, read at launch on Android 12 and newer. Changes with your wallpaper, and works with "Widget follows wallpaper" in light or dark.

  • Instant recolouring: Switch palettes in Spatch settings and the app updates on the spot, no restart.
  • Custom colours: Pick your own background, surface and accent colours when none of the 18 presets match.
  • Survives updates: Your theme and font choices stay intact when Spatch Manager updates your build.
