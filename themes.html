18 palettes, one tap

Every theme recolours the player, lists, cards and Spotify's own surfaces. Pick any chip below and this whole page switches to that palette, using the same 18 colour sets Spatch ships.

Wearing: the colours of this section

Switching applies straight away in Spatch settings or after a quick restart, and your choice stays intact when Spatch Manager updates your build.

Wallpaper colours, or your own

Spatch is not limited to presets. Follow your phone's wallpaper palette or set your own colours directly.

Wallpaper palette (Android 12+): Reads your phone's Material You palette at launch on Android 12 and newer. It changes with your wallpaper and works with “Widget follows wallpaper” in light or dark.

  • Instant recolouringSwitch palettes in Spatch settings and the app updates on the spot, without a restart.
  • Custom coloursPick your own background, surface and accent colours when none of the 18 presets match.
  • Cover-driven accentsIn the player, multi-colour covers can drive primary and secondary accents across the glow, bars and highlights.
  • Survives updatesYour theme and font choices stay intact when Spatch Manager updates your build.

Your font, everywhere

Pick one of the bundled families or import your own font files. Spotify's titles, lists and Spatch's own screens all follow it.

  • Spotify's ownDefault typography.
  • SystemYour Android font.
  • RobotoSans serif.
  • Noto SansSans serif.
  • Atkinson HyperlegibleSans serif, made for readability.
  • MonaspaceMonospace families: Neon, Argon, Xenon, Radon and Krypton.
  • Custom font filesImport your own font files from storage to use across the app.
